CCPA and SOC 2 notes (for Matt's approval)

  • CCPA is opt-out, not opt-in. It never required a banner. Our affirmative-accept standard goes beyond it, so the copy and layout carry no risk. The two things it does require: the "Do Not Sell or Share" link on the homepage (moving to the footer) and honoring Global Privacy Control. Both must be in place before the phrase leaves the banner. GPC was not found on the Design tab; it is on the Privacy tab or in Legislation presets and gets verified on ship day.
  • Symmetry rule. California's dark-pattern rules require declining to be as easy as accepting. Decline and Accept stay the same height on the first layer, one click each.
  • Nothing fires before a click. The dashboard type is "opt-in", the consent method is Explicit Consent, and that stays. Our own gate and the backend already require an affirmative Accept before anything forwards.
  • SOC 2: the wording is not a control, the change is. This is a production change to a privacy control made in a vendor dashboard. Matt's written approval is the change record. Dashboard screenshots from Oct 14 are the "before"; the same pages after saving are the "after". Both get filed in Thoropass.
  • Consent log unchanged. Accept and decline stay the recorded Cookiebot events, kept four years per the retention schedule. The privacy policy stays accurate with the footer link. Cookiebot stays in the vendor inventory. Rodolfo's nightly consent-gate check keeps running and gives the before-and-after consent rates.
  • Separate open item, not the banner: the Google Ads offline conversion import sends paid subscribers' email and name daily with no consent check. Michael flagged it Oct 8. Needs fixing under the same standard regardless of the banner.
